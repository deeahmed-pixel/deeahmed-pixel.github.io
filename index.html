The money

Your money goes straight to the supplier.
We never touch it.

The thing that worries people most about a renovation is what happens to the money for materials: prices nobody explains, commissions nobody mentions, and invoices that never arrive. We do it a completely different way.

  1. We call you at the moment of purchase

    Before anything is bought you get a call with the item, the quantity and the price.

  2. You transfer to the supplier yourself

    The money goes directly to the supplier, whoever they are. We are not part of the transfer and we take nothing from it.

  3. The tax invoice is in your name

    It comes to you from the supplier, showing the real prices that were actually paid.

  4. We charge supervision and execution

    One fee for supervision and execution, agreed in writing before work starts. That is all we charge, and there is nothing on top of it.

Want supply only?

We will sell you every material without installation, at the supplier's price, on a tax invoice in your name.

Not sure where to start?

Send us the details of the property and the budget you have. We will come back with a full costed schedule that gets the most out of it.

Why one contractor

Most renovations fail in the gaps between trades.

A premium project in Egypt usually runs through a stone supplier, a fabricator, a carpenter, an aluminium workshop, a blacksmith, an electrician and a contractor to argue with all of them. Every handover between them is a place for a delay, a wrong dimension, or a cost that appears at the end.

We own all of it. The block is bought by us, cut on our lines, matched to joinery built in our workshop, installed by our crews and wired by our electricians — from a single set of drawings, on one programme, for one number.

Defects cover on every handover

One contract instead of nine

Stone, joinery, glazing, shutters, ironwork and electrics all sit inside our business. There is no gap between trades for responsibility to fall through, and no week lost while two subcontractors argue about a level.

Coastal specification as standard

On the North Coast and in Alexandria we move to seaside-class coatings, 316 stainless, hot-dip galvanising and moisture-hardened cores — and we write it on the quotation so you can hold us to it.

Fixed prices against real specifications

We price a written specification, not a vague scope. Variations are quoted and approved before the work happens, never presented as a surprise at the end.

Built for owners who are not here

Weekly written reports with dated photographs, video walkthroughs at each stage and decisions batched into scheduled calls. A large share of our clients live in the Gulf, Europe or North America.

Honest material advice

We will tell you to put quartz on the worktop and keep the marble on the island face. We will tell you that quartz outdoors in Sahel is a mistake. Selling you the wrong stone twice is worse business than selling it right once.

Off-season coastal programming

We design in autumn and build through winter, so Sahel projects are finished before the season instead of during it — with easier access and better crew availability.

How it actually arrives

Our own material.
Our own trucks.

The reason a job runs to programme is rarely clever design. It is whether the stone was on the right lorry on the right morning. We buy it, cut it, load it and set it — so there is no third party to blame and nobody to wait for.

Stone leaves our yard

Slabs strapped upright and padded, cut and edged before they are loaded, so nothing is templated twice or trimmed on a client’s terrace.

We carry our own first fix

Pipe, conduit, boxes and fittings bought as a job lot and delivered by us. No half day lost because a merchant sent the wrong diameter.

Made to your measurements

Every panel leaves the workshop with its cut size written on the film. If a wall is out of square, that is settled at the bench, not with a grinder on site.

Questions we get asked

Before you call us.

What exactly does "from A to Z" mean?
That we are the only company on your contract. We source the stone at the block, cut it on our own lines, manufacture the kitchens, wardrobes, doors and metalwork in our own workshops, run the electrical and plumbing with our own crews, and install everything ourselves. You do not coordinate nine subcontractors, and there is nobody to blame when something does not line up — which is exactly the point.
Do you only do full renovations, or can I order just materials?
Both. Plenty of clients buy marble, granite or quartz from us supply-only, or take a single package such as a kitchen, the shutters or the staircase. The full turnkey contract is what we are best known for, but there is no minimum — if you need forty square metres of Galala cut and delivered, that is a normal order.
Where do you work in Egypt?
We are based in Alexandria, with showrooms in El Deresa. Our core regions are Alexandria, the North Coast, and Greater Cairo — especially New Cairo and the Fifth Settlement, Sheikh Zayed and the New Capital. We also work in El Gouna, Ain Sokhna and Marsa Matrouh. Everything is fabricated in our own workshops and installed by our own travelling crews.
How much does a project cost?
We do not publish per-metre numbers because they mislead. What drives a premium budget is stone, kitchens, bathrooms and joinery; plaster and paint are a small fraction of it. Send us drawings or let us survey, and you get a fixed, itemised quote broken down by trade — so you can see exactly what to add or cut.
Can you work with my architect or interior designer?
Regularly, and it usually produces the best results. We build to their design intent, produce the shop drawings, and flag early anything that will not manufacture or will not survive Egyptian humidity and coastal exposure. Designers tend to like working with us because the stone and the joinery come from the same set of drawings.
How do I start?
Send us drawings, photographs or just the address and a description. We reply within one working day, arrange a survey, and come back with a written specification and a fixed price. The survey and the quote cost you nothing.
