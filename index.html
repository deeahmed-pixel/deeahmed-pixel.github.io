The money

Your money goes straight to the supplier.
We never touch it.

The thing that worries people most about a renovation is what happens to the money for materials: prices nobody explains, commissions nobody mentions, and invoices that never arrive. We do it a completely different way.

  1. We call you at the moment of purchase

    Before anything is bought you get a call with the item, the quantity and the price.

  2. You transfer to the supplier yourself

    The money goes directly to the supplier, whoever they are. We are not part of the transfer and we take nothing from it.

  3. The tax invoice is in your name

    It comes to you from the supplier, showing the real prices that were actually paid.

  4. We charge supervision and execution

    One fee for supervision and execution, agreed in writing before work starts. That is all we charge, and there is nothing on top of it.

Want supply only?

We will sell you every material without installation, at the supplier's price, on a tax invoice in your name.

Not sure where to start?

Send us the details of the property and the budget you have. We will come back with a full costed schedule that gets the most out of it.

Questions people ask

Questions people ask us

Do you do full finishing only, or smaller jobs too?
Both. Many jobs are a kitchen, the bathrooms, the floors or a ceiling. Tell us which part, and the quotation covers only that.
Can I buy materials without installation?
Yes. Marble, granite, quartz and building materials are all available supply-only.
How do you price a job?
We do not publish a price per metre because it would not describe your unit. We visit, measure and give you an itemised quotation in writing.
Who pays for the materials?
You do, directly to the supplier, and the tax invoice is in your name. Our fee is for supervision and execution, agreed in writing before work starts.
Where are you?
Head office in El Ibrahimya and the showroom in El Deresa, Alexandria. Call or WhatsApp +20 106 926 2572.
