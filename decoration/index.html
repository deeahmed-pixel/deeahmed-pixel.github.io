Decoration

Decoration that is made, not ordered from a catalogue

Kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, ceilings, walls and stone, made in our own workshops and fitted by the people who made them.

Get a quotationWhatsApp

Decoration is where a finished apartment becomes your apartment: the kitchen you cook in, the ceiling you look up at, the wall behind the sofa, the stone underfoot. It is also where most of the money in a fit-out is spent, so it is where workmanship shows first.

We make the fitted pieces ourselves — kitchens, wardrobes, doors, vanities, stone tops — so the drawing, the cutting and the installation are one team’s responsibility. Drawings and samples are agreed before anything is made.

Modern, neo-classic or something of your own: the style is yours. Our job is that it is built straight, finished cleanly and still looks right in ten years.

Questions people ask

Do you design the decoration, or only build it?
Both. We agree drawings and samples with you before anything is made, and if you already have a designer, we build to their drawings.
Can I see materials and finishes before deciding?
Yes. Stone, finishes and samples can be seen at our showroom in El Deresa, Alexandria, and we bring samples to site when that is easier.
Which style suits a North Coast chalet?
Light, durable surfaces that can live with salt air and sand: honed or bush-hammered stone, powder-coated metals rated for the coast, and finishes that are easy to clean between seasons. We recommend by location, not by trend.
Can you redo one room only?
Yes. A kitchen, a bathroom, a bedroom wall or a ceiling on its own is a normal job for us.
