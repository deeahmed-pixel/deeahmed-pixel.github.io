Finishing

Finishing, from bare shell to keys

Apartments, villas, chalets and offices finished by one company. We supply the materials, make what is fitted in our own workshops, and hand over a home ready to live in.

Get a quotationWhatsApp

Finishing in Egypt usually means a dozen trades on one site — a plasterer, an electrician, a plumber, a tiler, a carpenter, a painter, a marble crew — each with their own supplier and their own idea of the schedule. We run them as one job, with one team, against one written quotation.

You can hand us the whole unit, from the bare shell to the day you move in, or one part of it: a kitchen, the bathrooms, the floors, a gypsum ceiling. The standard is the same either way.

And the money is never in our hands. On a finishing contract you pay each supplier directly and the tax invoice is issued in your name, so there is no margin hidden in the materials.

Questions people ask

What does full finishing include?
Everything between the bare shell and the day you move in: electrical and plumbing first fix, plaster and screed, floors, walls and ceilings, doors and windows, the kitchen and bathrooms, paint, and the final fit-out. The written quotation lists every item, so you can see exactly what is in and what is not.
Can you finish only part of the apartment?
Yes. Many jobs are a kitchen, the bathrooms, the floors or a gypsum ceiling in an apartment that is otherwise finished. Tell us which part, and the quotation covers only that.
How is the cost of finishing decided?
By the area, the level of finish you choose, the materials, and the state the unit is in today. We do not publish a price per metre because it would not describe your unit — we measure, ask, and give you an itemised quotation in writing.
Who buys the materials?
You do, directly. On a finishing contract you pay each supplier yourself and the tax invoice is in your name; we specify, check and install. Our fee is for supervision and execution.
Do you work outside Alexandria?
Yes. Alexandria is home, and we work across the North Coast and in New Cairo, and elsewhere in Egypt when the job is right for us.
