Sea-view Villa — Marble & Brass

Bare red-brick shell to a marble-and-brass interior. Book-matched marble on floors and walls, a white gloss kitchen with brass pendants over an LED-lit black island, backlit vanities, Nero Marquina and gold in the bathrooms, velvet channel headboards under cove-lit ceilings, and a white marble walkway banded in dark granite with an LED strip set into the joint.
