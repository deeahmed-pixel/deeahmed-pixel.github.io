The stone

About Egyptian Botticino.

A warm, close-grained beige with the faint parallel figure that the Italian original is known for, at a fraction of the landed cost. Specifiers working to a botticino palette use it as a like-for-like substitute in flooring and wall cladding, particularly in hospitality.
