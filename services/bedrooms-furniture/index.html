Frequently asked

Bedrooms & Furniture — questions we get asked

Do you make furniture to my own design?
Yes. Bring a drawing, a photograph or a reference from a brand you like. We produce shop drawings and a costed specification, tell you where the design will not survive Egyptian humidity or daily use, and build it. We do not copy a protected design outright, but we will interpret a look faithfully.
Veneer or lacquer for wardrobes?
Natural veneer if you want the room to feel warm and to age well — it can be re-finished. Lacquer if you want a specific colour or a matte, seamless look. In humid coastal units we lean toward veneer over a stable core, because sprayed finishes on a poorly-sealed edge are where the failures start.
Can you furnish a whole chalet before the season?
That is a large share of our spring work. Give us the drawings by late winter and we deliver and install a complete furniture package — bedrooms, wardrobes, media walls and outdoor pieces — before the season opens. We batch-manufacture in our workshop and install in one mobilisation.
Do you deliver outside Cairo?
Yes, throughout Egypt. Alexandria, Sahel, New Cairo, El Gouna and Ain Sokhna are routine runs for us. Furniture travels blanket-wrapped and assembled or flat-packed depending on the access, and our own team installs at the other end.
