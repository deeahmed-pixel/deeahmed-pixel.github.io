Marble

We buy marble at the block, not off a photograph. Our slabs are cut and calibrated on our own lines, book-matched in the yard where you can walk them, then templated and set by the same crew that measured the room.

Scope of works

What we actually do.

Everything below happens inside our business — no subcontracted links in the chain.

Get a quote for marble
  1. 01

    Sourcing at the block

    Direct relationships with quarries in Menia, Beni Suef and Suez, plus standing accounts with Carrara and Verona importers. You choose the block or the bundle; we photograph it, tag it and reserve it in your name.

  2. 02

    Cutting & finishing

    Gang-saw and bridge-saw cutting, calibration, resin treatment and finishing to polished, honed, brushed, leathered, bush-hammered, flamed or antiqued. Waterjet inlay and CNC profiling in-house.

  3. 03

    Book-matching & dry-lay

    Vein-matched panels laid out on the yard floor and photographed before a single piece leaves. You approve the layout, not a sample chip.

  4. 04

    Templating & installation

    Laser templating on site, mitred edges, hidden fixings, epoxy-anchored cladding systems and a joint schedule agreed in writing before we cut.

  5. 05

    Restoration & maintenance

    Diamond grinding, crystallisation, re-polishing, chip repair and impregnating sealers for stone that has already been laid — including work we did not install.

Frequently asked

Marble — questions we get asked

Is Egyptian marble good enough for a luxury villa?
For flooring and cladding, yes — Galala, Sunny and Silvia are exported worldwide and outperform many imported stones on hardness and price. We normally specify Egyptian stone for the large areas and reserve imported Calacatta or Statuario for the two or three surfaces people actually stand in front of. That mix is how you get a premium result without an imported-only budget.
How much does marble cost per square metre in Egypt?
Supplied and installed, Egyptian marble flooring typically lands in a wide band depending on stone, thickness and finish, while imported book-matched slabs sit several times higher. We do not publish a single number because edge details, wastage from vein matching and site access change it materially. Send us the drawings and you get a fixed, itemised quote — not a per-metre guess.
Will marble stain in a kitchen or bathroom?
Marble is calcium carbonate, so acids — lemon, vinegar, descaler — will etch it if left. We impregnate every piece before handover and hand you a care sheet. If you want zero maintenance around a cooking zone, we will tell you honestly to put quartz or sintered stone on the worktop and keep the marble on the island face and splashback.
Can you match marble I already have?
Usually. Send photos and, if possible, an offcut. We check our own stock and our supplier yards, and if the lot is gone we will show you the two closest alternatives side by side before you commit.
Do you deliver and install in the North Coast?
Yes. Sahel is a core service area for us — Marassi, Hacienda, Almaza Bay, Sidi Abdel Rahman and the New Alamein towers. We fabricate in our own workshops, transport on padded A-frames and install with our own crew, so nothing depends on a local subcontractor you have never met.
