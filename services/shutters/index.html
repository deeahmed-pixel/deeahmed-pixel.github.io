Shutters & Sun Control

In Sahel the sun is the main structural load on a room. Shutters are how you control it — heat, glare, privacy and security in one moving part. We make aluminium, PVC and timber systems, motorise them properly, and detail the box so it does not become the ugliest thing on the façade.

    Reinforced slats and locking end-rails for ground-floor and chalet units that stand empty out of season.

  6. 06

    Awnings & pergola shading

    Retractable awnings, bioclimatic louvred roofs and tensioned shade sails for terraces and pool decks.

Frequently asked

Shutters & Sun Control — questions we get asked

Which shutters survive the North Coast salt air?
Aluminium with a seaside-class powder-coat pretreatment and stainless steel fixings. Standard powder coating fails within a few seasons within a kilometre of the water — you get filiform corrosion under the paint at the edges. We specify the coastal grade as standard in Sahel and Alexandria, and we say so on the quotation so you can hold us to it.
Are motorised shutters worth it?
On anything above ground floor, on wide openings, and on any unit you leave empty — yes. Motors with a wind sensor also protect the shutter itself. Add a manual override crank so a power cut does not trap you behind a closed shutter, which is a detail cheap installations skip.
Can shutters be fitted to a finished villa?
Yes, with a surface-mounted box. It is visible, so we size and colour it to sit inside the reveal or align with the window head. A built-in box is invisible but has to be planned before plastering — which is why we prefer to be involved at the shell stage.
Do shutters actually reduce cooling costs?
Meaningfully. An external shutter stops solar gain before it reaches the glass, which an internal curtain cannot do. On a west-facing Sahel terrace the difference in a closed room through the afternoon is obvious without a meter.
