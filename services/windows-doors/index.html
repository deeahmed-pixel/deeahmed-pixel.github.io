Windows, Doors & Glazing

The window is the most expensive mistake in an Egyptian villa. Get the thermal break, the glass spec and the drainage right and the house is quiet and cheap to cool. Get them wrong and you have condensation, whistling and a cooling bill nobody can explain. We fabricate and install, so the survey, the glass order and the seal are one responsibility.

Selection

Systems and where they belong

  • Thermal-break casement & tilt-turnVillas

    The reliable default. Best seal, best security, easiest to service, and it takes a fly screen without looking added-on.

  • Lift-and-slideLarge openings

    Heavy panels that lift off their seal to glide, then drop to compress it. Quiet, weathertight, and the right system for a 4-metre terrace opening.

  • Minimal sightline slidersSea views

    When the client wants glass and not frame. Structurally demanding and worth specifying at the design stage.

  • uPVC multi-chamberValue & acoustics

    Outstanding thermal and sound numbers for the money. We use it where the frame will be curtained or the elevation is secondary.

  • Frameless glassBalustrades & screens

    Toughened laminated glass on channel or spigot fixings for terraces, stairs and pool edges.

Frequently asked

Windows & Doors — questions we get asked

Aluminium or uPVC in Egypt?
Aluminium for anything architectural — large spans, slim sightlines, sliding systems and coastal exposure — provided it has a genuine polyamide thermal break. uPVC gives better insulation per pound and excellent acoustics, but the frames are chunkier and colour choice is narrower. In practice we specify thermal-break aluminium on the main elevations and uPVC where budget matters more than the sightline.
Is double glazing worth it here?
Yes, and mostly for cooling rather than heating. A low-E, solar-control double-glazed unit rejects a large share of the heat that single glazing lets straight in, which lowers the AC load all summer. On a sea-facing Sahel elevation it is the single highest-return upgrade in the envelope, and it kills road and neighbour noise as a side effect.
What fails first on coastal windows?
The hardware and the coating, not the glass. Standard steel fittings seize and standard powder coat blisters within a few kilometres of the sea. We use stainless coastal-grade hardware and seaside-class pretreatment, and we drain and vent the frames properly, because trapped water inside a profile is what destroys a window from the inside.
Can you replace windows in a finished apartment?
Yes. We survey, fabricate, then remove and replace opening by opening — usually one room per day — with the reveals made good and painted. We work around occupied homes regularly, including tower units in Alexandria and New Cairo with lift and access restrictions.
