Frequently asked

Steel & Ironwork — questions we get asked

How do you stop steel rusting in Sahel?
Specification, not paint. Anything exposed within a few kilometres of the sea gets hot-dip galvanising before powder coating, or 316 stainless where the section allows. Fixings are stainless, welds are ground and re-protected, and drainage holes go in every hollow section so water cannot sit inside. Painting bare mild steel on the coast buys you about two seasons.
Can you build a cantilevered staircase in an existing house?
Often, but it depends entirely on what the treads are anchored into. A cantilever needs a wall or spine that can take a large moment, so we start with a structural survey and calculations. If the wall cannot take it we will show you a spine-beam version that looks similar and is honest about the physics.
Do you do wrought iron in the traditional style?
Yes — hand-forged, not cast. Scrolls, twists, collars and hammered texture done at the anvil. It costs more than bolted-together cast components and it is immediately recognisable as the real thing, particularly on gates and stair balustrades in classical villas.
Are your pergolas engineered for wind?
Every one. Coastal terraces and roof levels see wind loads that people badly underestimate, and an under-designed pergola is a genuine hazard. We calculate the frame, size the base plates and specify the fixings into real structure — never into screed or tile.
