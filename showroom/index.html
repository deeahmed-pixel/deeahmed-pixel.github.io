Our marble and granite showroom in El Deresa

Stone is not chosen from a photograph. At our showroom in El Deresa, Alexandria, you see the stone in person, compare finishes and talk it through with our team, drawings in hand.

What you can do at the showroom

The showroom is a place to make decisions, not just to browse. What you can do there:

  • See stone in person: the real colour, veining and texture, which photos and screens never show accurately.
  • Compare finishes: polished, honed and textured surfaces, and which suits a floor, a wall or a kitchen.
  • Bring your drawings and measurements and go through them with our team, so the conversation is about where each stone goes in your home, not about the stone alone.
  • Talk to our team about thickness, edges and installation, and about supply-only or supply with installation.

What is on show changes, so if you have a particular stone from the catalogue in mind, name it in your message before you come and ask whether you can see it.

How to choose stone in person

Natural stone never repeats, and two slabs of the same stone can differ in colour and veining. These are the points that matter when you choose:

  1. Look at the whole slab, not the sample

    A small sample shows the colour only. A full slab shows how the veining moves and spreads, and any spots or variation. Choose on the largest piece you can see.

  2. Check the veining and its direction

    Decide whether you want quiet or bold veining, and whether it should run one way across the room. For a TV wall or a bathroom you may need consecutive slabs from the same block to make a book-match.

  3. Compare finishes on the same stone

    The same stone changes colour and feel between polished, honed, brushed or bush-hammered. Ask for finish samples and compare them side by side.

  4. Ask about thickness

    The right thickness differs between floors, walls, stairs and kitchen worktops. Ask what thickness is available in the stone you chose and what suits each use.

  5. Ask about edges and details

    Square, bevelled, half-round or full-round edges, mitred corners, stair nosings, and cut-outs for the sink and hob are all agreed before cutting.

  6. See it under more than one light

    Marble looks different in daylight and under warm artificial light. Look at it under both, and think about the light in the room where it will go.

Finishes compared

Choosing the finish matters as much as choosing the stone. A practical summary:

FinishLookSuitsWatch out for
PolishedReflects light and shows colour stronglyWalls and quieter indoor floorsShows scratches and wear more, and can be slippery when wet
HonedSmooth, with no shineFloors, bathrooms and modern interiorsCan take stains more readily; may need a sealer depending on the stone
Bush-hammered, or flamed (granite)Rough to the touchOutdoor areas and around poolsHarder to clean than smooth surfaces
Brushed or leatheredSoft, gently undulating textureFeature walls and kitchen worktopsThe texture holds dirt if it is not cleaned properly

What to bring on your visit

  • Drawings or a floor plan of the unit, even hand-drawn, with measurements.
  • Measurements of where the stone will go: floors, stairs, kitchen worktops, bathrooms, the TV wall.
  • Photos of the space as it is today, and of ideas you like.
  • Samples of other materials in the home (a wardrobe door, a paint colour, a tile) to compare against the stone.
  • A shortlist of the stones you liked in the catalogue on our site.

Plan your visit, step by step

  1. Browse the catalogue

    Start with the catalogue on our site: 94 stones (marble, granite, quartz, onyx and semi-precious stone) with their names, origins and finishes. Make a shortlist.

  2. Message or call

    WhatsApp or call +20 106 926 2572, name the stones you want to see and what you are planning, and agree a time within showroom hours.

  3. Come with your drawings

    Compare stones and finishes in person, and go through sizes and details with our team.

  4. Get a written quotation

    Once the stone, sizes and details are settled, you get a written, itemised quotation, for supply only or for supply with installation.

Itmam: from the showroom to installation

Itmam is a finishing, decoration and supply company, with its head office in El Ibrahimya and its showroom in El Deresa, Alexandria. You can buy stone supply-only, or have us template, cut and install it: marble and granite are templated, cut and installed by the same team, and stone tops, kitchens, wardrobes, doors and vanities are made in our own workshops.

We work in Alexandria, on the North Coast and in New Cairo and the Fifth Settlement, and elsewhere in Egypt when the job suits. On a finishing contract you pay each supplier directly and the tax invoice is in your name; our fee for supervision and execution is agreed in writing before work starts. We have been in this business for 20+ years.

Questions people ask

Where is the showroom and when is it open?
In El Deresa, Alexandria; our head office is in El Ibrahimya. Hours are Saturday to Thursday, 09:00 to 18:00. To confirm before you come, call or WhatsApp +20 106 926 2572.
Do I need an appointment?
It is best to get in touch before you visit. A WhatsApp message or a call is enough to tell us what you want to see, so the visit focuses on what matters to you, especially if you want to see a particular stone from the catalogue.
Can I choose the stone myself?
Yes; that is the reason to come. You see the stone in person and compare veining and finishes before you decide. Name the stones you are interested in when you message us, and ask what can be seen on the day of your visit.
Do you sell marble without installation?
Yes. Supply-only is available for marble, granite and quartz. If you want it installed, the same team templates, cuts and installs it.
Can I bring my apartment drawings?
Yes, and it helps. Drawings and measurements turn the conversation to where each stone goes, its thickness and its edges, and they help us prepare a written, itemised quotation.
Do you work outside Alexandria?
Yes. We work in Alexandria, on the North Coast and in New Cairo and the Fifth Settlement, and elsewhere in Egypt when the job suits. Tell us where the site is when you ask for a quotation.
