Building materials supply in Alexandria, from cement to tiles

Building materials supply in Alexandria goes well when you know exactly what to order, what to check the moment it arrives, and where to keep it in damp sea air. Here are the materials we supply, from cement and rebar to tiles, plumbing and electrical materials, and how an order runs from quantities to a written quotation. Itmam is a finishing and building-materials supply company from Alexandria: buy the material on its own, or as part of a finishing contract.

Structural materials: what to order and what to check on arrival

Check structural materials on the lorry and before you sign the delivery note, because a fault is hard to prove once the material is on site or in the concrete.

MaterialWhat to state when orderingWhat to check on arrival
CementThe type and class in your specification: ordinary, blended, sulphate-resisting, white or masonry, and the number of bagsThe type and class printed on the bag, the production date, bags intact and dry with no hard lumps when pressed, and the count matching the delivery note
RebarDiameters, lengths and grade from the structural drawings, and the bending schedule if you want it cut and bentThe markings on the bars and the tag on each bundle, the mill test certificate for the delivery, the diameter with a caliper, and the weight: kg per metre = the diameter in mm squared ÷ 162. Light surface rust is normal; flaking scale or pitting is not
Bricks and blocksType and size: red brick, hollow or solid cement block, or lightweight block, and the countEven sizes, few broken pieces, red brick evenly fired with no cracks, and blocks with sound edges that do not crumble
Sand and aggregateWashed sand for concrete and plaster, or fill sand, the aggregate size, and the volume in m³Clean sand for mortar: a jar test should show only a thin layer of silt. No sea sand in anything with steel in it. Measure the lorry body to check the volume, and remember that damp sand bulks up and looks like more than it is

Finishing materials: tiles, paint, sanitaryware and electrical supplies

Most problems with finishing materials are a difference between what you ordered and what arrived: a shade, a batch, a size or a model, so check before anything is opened or fitted.

MaterialWhat to state when orderingWhat to check on arrival
Ceramic and porcelain tilesType, size and grade, the quantity with wastage, and the adhesive and grout to go with themThe same shade code and size code on every carton, the grade you ordered, and sound cartons. Open several cartons and compare tiles side by side
PaintThe whole system: sealer, filler and paint, the colour code, and the quantity from the coverageOne batch number for each colour, sealed tins, the production date, and the colour code matching
Plumbing and sanitarywareThe pipe system, pressure class and sizes, the fittings list, and the sanitaryware modelsClass and size printed on the pipe, pipes and fittings from one system, and sanitaryware unboxed and checked for chips and cracks before you sign
Electrical materialsCable sizes and lengths, the board, breaker ratings, switches and socketsThe cross-section printed on the cable sheath, a conductor of the right size (solid 2.5 mm² copper is about 1.78 mm across), and breaker ratings matching the list
Marble, granite and quartzThe stone, thickness and finish, after you have seen it at our showroom in El Deresa, Moharram BeyColour and veining matching the slab you chose, thickness and finish as agreed, and no cracks

Turn away anything that differs from the order before it is unloaded, and write your remark on the delivery note.

The price of cement and steel today? What moves the price of an order

We do not publish prices, because building-material prices move from week to week and a figure on a page would not describe your order. What sets the price of any order:

  • The type, grade and producer: every type of cement, grade of steel and grade of tile has its own price.
  • The quantity and the packing: bags or bulk, cartons or pallets.
  • The market on the day, especially for steel and cable, which follow metal prices.
  • The site and the access: street width, the floor, whether there is a lift, and who unloads and carries up.
  • Extra work, such as cutting and bending rebar to a schedule.

How an order works: from quantities to a written quotation

  1. Send the quantities

    Quantities, a bill of quantities (BOQ), drawings, or a photo of your list on WhatsApp. If you have not worked them out yet, the building-materials calculator gives bricks, cement and sand for walls, plaster and tile beds; the tile and paint calculators give tiles and paint; and the finishing-cost-by-size guide estimates a whole flat’s quantities from its area.

  2. Get a written quotation

    Itemised, for exactly what you asked for, not a price list.

  3. Supply-only: approve and pay the supplier

    You approve the quotation and pay the supplier, with the invoice in your name, and you see the material and the invoice before it moves.

  4. Or within a finishing contract

    If the materials are part of finishing your flat, we visit, measure and write the quantities into the finishing quotation, and you pay each supplier directly at their price.

  5. Check on arrival

    Check the material against the two tables above before you sign for it.

Sea air: store materials properly

Alexandria’s damp, salty air spoils badly stored material before it is used, so storage is part of the order, not an afterthought.

  • Cement: it draws moisture from the air itself. Keep the bags off the ground on timber or pallets, away from external walls, covered with a sheet, in low stacks, and use the oldest first. If storage space is tight, order in batches that follow the pace of the work.
  • Rebar: on timber bearers off the ground, away from sand piles and standing water, and covered near the sea, because salt air quickly turns light rust into pitting.
  • Tiles: cartons in a dry, covered place, because a wet carton falls apart and the tiles break when moved.
  • Paint, cables and pipes: inside the flat or in a locked store, out of direct sun.

Sulphate-resisting cement belongs in concrete that touches salty ground or groundwater, such as footings, ground beams and buried tanks, and the decision is your structural engineer’s, in the specification. For mortar, plaster and tile beds, use the type named in your project’s specification.

Narrow streets and upper floors without a big lift

In older buildings and narrow streets, getting material up to the flat needs arranging before you order, not on the day it arrives.

  • Unloading: ask the building management and the neighbours where a vehicle can stop to unload, and at what time of day, so the street is not blocked.
  • The lift: find out whether it may be used for materials, measure its door and car, and note the load printed in it.
  • Carrying up the stairs: agree in advance who carries the bags and cartons, to which floor, and how the stairs and entrance are protected.
  • Large tiles and marble slabs: measure the stair turns and the flat’s door before choosing a large format, because a piece that cannot go up in one piece changes the choice, or its joints must be set from the start.
  • Sand: agree where it is tipped, or whether it is bagged, and cover it so wind and rain do not carry it off.

Write these arrangements down with the order, so you know what the quotation includes and what it does not: loading, transport, unloading and carrying up to the floor.

Questions people ask

What is the price of cement in Alexandria today?
We do not print prices on the website, because the price of cement moves and today’s figure will not be next week’s. The price depends on the type, class and producer, the quantity, bags or bulk, and where the site is and which floor. Send the type, the quantity and the site location, and you get a written quotation for your order.
Do you supply sanitaryware and electrical materials in Alexandria?
Yes, supply-only with no installation contract: pipes, fittings, valves, bathroom sets and water heaters; cable, boards, breakers, switches, sockets and lighting. Send the list, the bathroom drawing or the circuit list, and we go through the take-off with you so the small fittings people forget arrive with the order.
Can I buy building materials from you without a finishing contract?
Yes, supply-only is a normal order for us. You get a written quotation for exactly what you asked for, you pay the supplier with the invoice in your name, and you see the material and the invoice before it moves.
What is a contracting and supply company, and how should I deal with one?
A company that supplies materials and carries out the work as well. What matters is knowing who buys the material and whose name is on the invoice. On our finishing contracts you pay each supplier directly at their price, the tax invoice is in your name, and our management fee is agreed in writing; if you only want the material, supply-only is available.
How do I store cement and rebar in Alexandria’s climate?
Cement off the ground on timber, away from external walls, covered with a sheet, oldest first. Rebar on bearers off the ground and covered, away from sand and water. If storage space is small, order in batches.
Do I need sulphate-resisting cement in Alexandria?
Concrete that touches salty ground or groundwater, such as footings and buried tanks, may need it, and the decision is your structural engineer’s, in the specification. Mortar, plaster and tile beds take the type named in your project’s specification. If you are not sure, send us the specification or the drawings.
