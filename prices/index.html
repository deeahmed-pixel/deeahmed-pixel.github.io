Cost

Renovation cost in Egypt,
and what actually sets it.

Anyone who gives you a number for a renovation before seeing the unit is guessing. We do not print prices on this site, and we tell you why. What you will find here is the list of things that genuinely decide the cost — read it before you ask anyone for a quote, us included.

Levels

Four levels of finish,
and the difference is in the work itself.

Economy finish

Plaster, ceramic floors, emulsion paint, standard doors and windows, standard electrical and plumbing. Right for a rental or an investment unit.

Lux finish

Porcelain or local marble in the reception, gypsum board with concealed lighting, a better grade of kitchen and bathrooms, branded electrical fittings.

Super lux

Marble in the reception and bathrooms, joinery made to measure, designed gypsum work and lighting, concealed air conditioning, branded sanitaryware and mixers.

Villas and special work

Carved pieces, book-matched marble, lifts, swimming pools, landscape. Here the job is priced from the drawing, not from the square metre.

These levels describe the work, not its price. We do not print prices because any figure written today is wrong within a week as steel, cement and the dollar move — and a printed figure makes people compare things that are not alike. Want a number for your own unit? A site visit and a written quote, itemised line by line.

What matters

What actually moves the price.

These are the items that move an apartment from one range to the next. Anyone who quotes without asking about them is guessing.

  1. The state of the unit before you start. A core-and-shell unit is not the same job as one with an old finish that has to come out. Demolition and removal alone is a costed line before anything is fitted.
  2. How many bathrooms and kitchens. These are the most expensive square metres in any home. A two-bathroom apartment can cost more than a larger one with a single bathroom — which is why pricing by the metre alone misleads.
  3. Floors. The difference between local ceramic and Galala marble over the same area is large. Flooring is usually the single biggest line in a finish.
  4. Electrical and plumbing. Every moved socket or outlet means chasing walls. Changing the lighting layout is far cheaper decided before plastering than after it.
  5. Air conditioning. Concealed ducted AC needs ceiling height, gypsum board and access hatches. It costs clearly more than split units, and it has to be decided early.
  6. Joinery. A kitchen and wardrobes made to measure are built in a workshop. Off the shelf is cheaper; made to measure uses the space better and lasts longer.
  7. Time. Urgency is paid for. A reasonable programme costs less than a compressed one, because compression means more labour and extra shifts.

Materials

Supply pricing.

How materials are priced
Egyptian marble (Galala, Sunny, Silvia)By the square metre — grade, thickness and finish decide it
Imported marble (Statuario, Nero Marquina)By the square metre — by the block, the vein and the quantity
Egyptian granite (Aswan, Halayeb, Ghazal)By the square metre — colour, size and finish
CementBy the tonne or the bag — moves weekly
Reinforcement steelBy the tonne — moves daily; we price against your bar schedule
Sand, gravel and brickBy the cubic metre or the thousand — distance to site is part of the sum

We do not print prices here. Cement and steel change faster than any web page, and marble is priced from the block itself and from the quantity. Send us the quantity, the bar schedule or the marble sizes, and today's price comes back to you in writing.

Questions we get asked

Questions about cost

How much does finishing cost per metre in Egypt?
There is no single number, and anyone who gives you one before seeing the unit is guessing. The price is set by the state of the unit, the number of bathrooms and kitchens, the type of floor, and the level of joinery and air conditioning. We visit, measure and issue a written quote itemised line by line — that is the figure you work to.
Why are there no prices on the site?
Because a printed figure misleads. Steel and cement move, and two apartments of the same size can differ a great deal by bathrooms and materials. A number on a website makes people compare things that are not alike, and then be surprised. We would rather survey and write it down.
How much deposit do you take?
Payments are tied to delivered stages, not to dates. You pay against a stage that is finished and inspected, and that schedule is written into the contract before we start.
Can I buy the materials myself and have you install?
You can, and we often work that way. Most clients let us arrange it instead: we give you the item, the quantity and the supplier, you pay the supplier directly, and the tax invoice is in your name. Either way we check the material before it goes in.
Does the price change during the work?
No. What we agreed is written. Any change you ask for is written up as a change order with its price before it is carried out. Nobody is surprised at the end.
