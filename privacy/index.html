Privacy note.

Short, and in plain language.

What we collect. Only what you send us: your name, phone number, email if you give one, the property location and whatever you write in the message. We do not buy contact lists and we do not scrape them.

What we use it for. Replying to your enquiry, arranging a survey and quoting your project. Nothing else.

Who sees it. Our own team. We do not sell or rent your details. Where a project needs a specialist partner — a pool contractor, for example — we share only what that partner needs, and only after telling you.

Analytics and advertising. This site may use Meta Pixel and Google Analytics to measure which pages lead to enquiries. These set cookies and record page views in aggregate. They are configured to measure marketing performance, not to build profiles of individuals for resale.

How long we keep it. Active enquiries and project records for as long as the commercial relationship and any warranty period requires. Ask us to delete your details and we will.

Contact. Write to Sales@Eliterenovations.com for anything on this page, including a request to see or delete what we hold.
