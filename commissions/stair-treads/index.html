Stair treads & helical stringers

درج رخام

A straight flight is measurement. A curved or helical flight is a template job: every tread is a different shape and they are cut from a survey of the actual structure, not from the architect's drawing, because the two are never quite the same.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

Including the ones that curve, which is a different job entirely.

  1. The built structure is templated in full, then treads are nested and cut individually. No two are assumed identical.
  2. Nosings run to a matching profile around the curve, which is the detail that separates a good stone stair from an expensive one.
  3. Risers, skirtings and the raking stringer are cut from the same slab sequence so the colour walks up the flight instead of jumping.
Stair treads & helical stringers specification
TemplateFull site template of the built structure
TreadsCut individually; curved flights have no repeats
FinishHoned on wet routes, polished internally
Lead time5 to 8 weeks depending on flight

Worth knowing before you order

Template after the structure is built and before the plaster. Templating too early is the single most common cause of a re-cut.

What it is cut from.

Honed rather than polished on any stair that gets wet feet. A polished marble tread by a pool is a hospital visit waiting to happen.
