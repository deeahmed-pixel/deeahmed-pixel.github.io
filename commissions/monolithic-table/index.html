Commission 06 · Tables, consoles & seating

Dining table on a stone base

ترابيزة سفرة رخام

The base is the engineering. A stone top two and a half metres long has to be carried at the right two points or it will crack across the middle in its own good time — usually about eighteen months after you paid for it.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

A top that spans, on a base that does not apologise for its size.

  1. Top is 20 mm slab mitred to read 60–100 mm, or 30 mm solid where the span is short enough to allow it.
  2. The base is either a pair of solid blades, a single central drum, or a mitred box. Blades and drums are set by calculation, not by eye.
  3. A concealed steel spine goes under any top over 2.2 m. It is invisible and it is the difference between a table and a future insurance claim.
Dining table on a stone base specification
Typical size2000–3000 mm long, 900–1100 mm wide
Top20 mm mitred to 60–100 mm, or 30 mm solid
BaseTwin blades, central drum, or mitred box
ReinforcementConcealed steel spine over 2.2 m span

Worth knowing before you order

Marble tops and red wine. We seal, and we still recommend granite or quartzite for a table that will be used rather than admired.
