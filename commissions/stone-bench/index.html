Commission 08 · Tables, consoles & seating

Bench, plinth & seat

مقعد حجري

A seat in solid stone is one of the few pieces of furniture that is genuinely permanent. Outdoors it wants a granite; in a steam room it wants a stone that will not spall when it is soaked and heated a thousand times.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

For a hall, a hammam, a pool edge or a garden that stays out all year.

  1. Solid slab seat on two solid legs, mitred to hide the joint, or a single cored block for short spans.
  2. Outdoor pieces are set on a compacted, drained base — a bench that settles unevenly cracks at the leg.
  3. For steam and hammam we use a granite or a dense marble and we bed with a flexible adhesive, not a rigid one.
Bench, plinth & seat specification
Typical size1200–2000 mm long, 420–450 mm high
Seat40–60 mm solid, or 20 mm mitred to depth
OutdoorGranite, on a compacted drained base
Lead time3 to 4 weeks

Worth knowing before you order

Dark granite in full Sahel sun gets hot enough to be unusable at midday. Pale stone or shade.
