Solid stone bath

بانيو من الحجر

A freestanding bath carved from block, or built from mitred panels around a shell where weight has to come down. Both look the same from a metre away. Only one of them can go on the first floor of most villas.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

The heaviest thing that will ever go into the room.

  1. Carved: a single block hollowed on the CNC and hand-finished. Two and a half tonnes of block for a bath that finishes near 700 kg, empty.
  2. Panelled: 20 mm slab mitred around a structural shell, finishing near 180 kg empty. Same face, a quarter of the load, roughly a third of the price.
  3. Either way the bath goes in before the door frame. This is not a detail we can work around later.
Solid stone bath specification
CarvedFrom block, ~700 kg empty, ~950 kg filled
PanelledMitred slab on a shell, ~180 kg empty
Typical size1700 × 800 × 550 mm
Lead time8 to 10 weeks carved, 5 weeks panelled

Worth knowing before you order

Floor loading, access, and the order of works. We survey the route from the street to the room before we quote a carved bath, and we have walked away from jobs where the stair could not take it.

Stone

What it is cut from.

Stone holds heat far longer than acrylic, which is the actual reason to want one. It also draws heat from the first fill, so run it hot.
