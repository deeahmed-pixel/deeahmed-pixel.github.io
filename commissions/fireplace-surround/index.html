Commission 12 · Architectural stonework

Fireplace surround

مدفأة رخام

From a plain mitred slab opening to a carved bolection with a moulded shelf. The constraint is heat: the stone nearest the firebox expands, and if it is trapped by the stone around it, it cracks.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

The one piece in the room everyone faces.

  1. Movement joints designed in at the firebox, filled with a heat-tolerant flexible sealant that is the colour of the stone.
  2. Mouldings run on the CNC then hand-worked, because a machine-only moulding has a deadness to it at this scale.
  3. Fixed back to a frame, never bonded directly to blockwork that will move.
Fireplace surround specification
Typical size1200–1800 mm wide, 1100–1400 mm high
BuildSlab or carved bolection, on a fixing frame
HeatMovement joints at the firebox as standard
Lead time5 to 7 weeks

Worth knowing before you order

Tell us the appliance before we draw. A wood burner and a gas feature need different clearances and different stone.

Stone

What it is cut from.

For a working fire, granite or a dense marble. Onyx and alabaster are for a gas feature or an ornamental opening only.
