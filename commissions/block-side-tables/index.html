Commission 07 · Tables, consoles & seating

Block side and coffee tables

ترابيزات جانبية

Cylinders, cubes, drums and stepped blocks cut from the ends of slabs and the last of a block. Because the material is already paid for by another job, this is the cheapest way to own a piece of a stone you could never floor a room in.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

Offcuts, which is the only reason these are affordable.

  1. Cored, sawn or mitred from remnant. We show you what is actually on the rack rather than a rendering of what you might get.
  2. Solid where the size allows, mitred hollow where it would otherwise be unliftable.
  3. Felt or cork to the base as standard, because stone on marble flooring writes on it.
Block side and coffee tables specification
Typical size350–600 mm across, 380–450 mm high
BuildSolid where liftable, mitred hollow above
BaseFelt or cork faced as standard
Lead time2 weeks, subject to remnant stock

Worth knowing before you order

Remnant means what it says. If you want six matching, tell us early and we will cut them from one slab.

Stone

What it is cut from.

This is where the semi-precious materials make sense. A side table in petrified wood or picture jasper costs a fraction of what the same stone would cost as a wall.
