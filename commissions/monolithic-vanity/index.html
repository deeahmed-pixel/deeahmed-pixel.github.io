Commission 02 · Basins, vanities & baths

Monolithic vanity, basin carved in

وحدة حوض من كتلة واحدة

The purest version of a stone vanity and the most expensive, because most of the block ends up on the floor as dust. The top, the bowl, the splashback and the returns are one continuous piece of stone with no joint anywhere, so the vein runs unbroken from the wall, across the deck, down into the bowl and out again.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

One block. The basin is not fitted to it, it is taken out of it.

  1. A block is squared, then the bowl is roughed out on the five-axis CNC and finished by hand. Between 55 and 70 per cent of the block is removed.
  2. The bowl is cut with a fall to the waste built into the carve, not chased in afterwards, so water never stands in the base.
  3. The whole piece is impregnated before it leaves the workshop and again after installation. On a marble bowl this is not optional — see the note below.

Typical size900–1800 mm wide, 500–550 mm deep

Worth knowing before you order

Marble is calcium carbonate. Lemon, vinegar, descaler and some cleaning products will etch it, sealed or not. If the bathroom belongs to a family that will not think about it, we will steer you to granite or to a quartz deck with a stone-look surround.

Stone

What it is cut from.

Fine, even-grained creams carve cleanly and hold a crisp arris. Anything with a fossil bed or a hard vein running through it will chip at the bowl rim, which is why we will sometimes refuse a stone you have already fallen in love with.
