Commission 15 · Light, water & the decorative

Backlit onyx & agate panels

ألواح أونكس مضيئة

Translucent stone lit from behind is the effect every hotel lobby wants and most get wrong, because they light it with points instead of a plane. Done properly you see the geology. Done badly you see the LEDs.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

The stone stops being a surface and becomes the light.

  1. Stone is calibrated down to 8–12 mm and laminated to a glass or acrylic backing. Below that thickness onyx will not survive handling.
  2. Lit with an edge-lit panel or a dense LED sheet at a set stand-off — never with spots, which print themselves through as hot dots.
  3. The panel is mocked up and lit in the workshop before it is cut to size. Every block of onyx transmits differently and no sample predicts it.
Backlit onyx & agate panels specification
Thickness8–12 mm calibrated, laminated to glass or acrylic
LightingEdge-lit panel or LED sheet at set stand-off

Worth knowing before you order

Heat and colour temperature both change what you see. We set the light with you rather than picking a number off a datasheet.
