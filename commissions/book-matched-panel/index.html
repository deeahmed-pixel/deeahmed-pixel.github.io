Commission 14 · Architectural stonework

Book-matched & quad-matched panels

ألواح متطابقة

Two slabs cut one after the other off the same block, opened and set side by side, mirror each other along the joint. Four of them make a butterfly that closes on a centre line. It is the single most dramatic thing you can do with stone and it has to be bought as a bundle at the block, not picked from a rack later.

Get a price for this Ask on WhatsApp

How it is built

Consecutive slabs opened like a book so the vein mirrors.

  1. Slabs are reserved in sequence at the block. Once a bundle is broken it can never be reassembled.
  2. Dry-laid in the yard, photographed and numbered, and you approve the layout before a fixing is cut.
  3. Fixed on a mechanical frame for anything above head height. Adhesive alone is not a fixing for a full-height panel.
Book-matched & quad-matched panels specification
SupplyReserved in sequence at the block, as a bundle
ApprovalDry-laid, numbered and photographed for sign-off
FixingMechanical frame above head height

Worth knowing before you order

Decide early. A book-match cannot be added to a job once the block has been cut for somebody else.
